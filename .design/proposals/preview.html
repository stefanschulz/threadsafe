ThreadSafe – logo proposals

Grammar of SetLister: graphite tile #1b232c, brass #e0a13d, one outlined shape and one brass disc with a graphite rim that steps out of it.

Round 2 – with the backup meaning

4 · Safe + restore (icon reworked)

icon: 16, 24 and 48 px

The safe with hinges and dial (the backup is kept safe) and the restore arrow with clock hands. The icon shows both as outlines, open towards each other as SetLister’s rows end before its disc; for 16 px there is a simpler variant (4-safe-restore-icon-simple.svg).

5 · Archive box + backup

icon: 16, 24 and 48 px

An archive box (lid and handle slot as bars) and the disc with the arrow into the tray: back up, save.

6 · ZIP: page with zipper

icon: 16, 24 and 48 px

A backup is a standard ZIP archive: the page with the zipper, and the pull of the zipper is the disc.
